---
name: Analyze Admission Materials
description: Extracts and structures key information from university admission materials, study guides, and counseling results to provide personalized study directions.
---

# Instructions

When analyzing admission materials:
1. Identify the source of the material (e.g., Essay Guidebook, Counseling Result, Admission Info Session).
2. Extract key metrics: test scores, expected admission cutoffs, competition ratios, and key dates.
3. For counseling results, summarize the student's current status, identified weaknesses, and the counselor's recommended action plan.
4. For essay guidebooks, extract the exam format, duration, word count limits, preferred topics, and assessment criteria.
5. Synthesize these points into a structured markdown output, separating 'Factual Information' from 'Actionable Advice'.
